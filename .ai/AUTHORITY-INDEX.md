# Bluewave Authority Index

Status: active register, established 2026-10-15 by the G-1 sprint under ADR-0210
(decision DR-010). It is a ledger/index that grows by design, so it is not word-budgeted.

This is the map of what is authoritative in this repository, what is unavailable, and
what replaces it. When a document cites an artifact that is not here, treat it as an
unidentified authority and stop. Raw evidence and the full enumeration are in
[`.ai/reports/g-1-artifact-inventory.md`](reports/g-1-artifact-inventory.md).

## Authority order

Per ADR-0210 (DR-010):

1. Accepted decisions — the ADR corpus (`docs/adr/`) and the records below.
2. Roadmap and plan — [`.ai/ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md), [`.ai/SPRINT-PLAN.md`](SPRINT-PLAN.md).
3. The RTW3 manual — `docs/Rule the Waves 3 Manual EBOOK.pdf`, except where a decision
   deliberately diverges.
4. Tests.
5. Production content (`content/`).
6. Implementation — describes behaviour, not intent.
7. New cited research — `docs/research/`.
