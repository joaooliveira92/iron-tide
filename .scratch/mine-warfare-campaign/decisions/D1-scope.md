# D1: Scope

**Type:** grilling · **Status:** resolved

## Question
Which of the manual's three mine representations does this effort deliver?

## Answer
Only the monthly **operational** layer (p.74): ships equipped to lay add to an
area's mine value, sweepers add to its sweeping value, and the comparison drives
the risk of strikes during operational movement. The pre-battle mining round and
in-battle minefields need a player-fought battle and tactical fidelity (T-2, T-3)
and are deferred; base defensive minefields need movement rules and are deferred.

## Why
Only the operational layer has a campaign trigger today and passes the gate
criterion (a deterministic compiled-month test observes authoritative state).
