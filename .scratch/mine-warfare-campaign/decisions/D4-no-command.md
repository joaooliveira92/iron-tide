# D4: No player command

**Type:** grilling · **Status:** resolved

## Question
Does the player order mine laying?

## Answer
No. The manual says equipped ships "will be assumed to carry out mining in the
area they are deployed"; operational mining is abstracted. The player's levers
are design, construction and deployment. A laying command would diverge from the
source game and is not added.

## Why
Simulation behaviour must match RTW3; only UI/UX may deviate.
