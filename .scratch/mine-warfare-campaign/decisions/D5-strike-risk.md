# D5: Strike risk

**Type:** grilling · **Status:** resolved

## Question
How does pressure affect ships, given the engine's route risk is value-only?

## Answer
For a ship ending operational movement in or through a covered area, the existing
route-risk value (pressure, freshness, exposure, susceptibility) is compared with
a recorded domain-separated draw keyed to ship, area and month; a strike applies
the engine's strike outcome as damage and emits a mine incident report. Risk never
denies movement (the engine contract forbids auto-deny). Susceptibility inputs
(for example screening small ships) use only values the engine already supplies;
anything else stays in the fog.

## Why
The manual: pressure increases "the risk of opposing ships falling victims to
mines" during operational movement.
