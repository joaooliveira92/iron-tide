# D6: Knowledge

**Type:** grilling · **Status:** resolved

## Question
What does the player know?

## Answer
Authoritative pressure is never shown directly for enemy-laid mines. The player
sees pressure from their own nation's laying and enemy mines only through
incident reports (immutable, estimated cause, confidence, delay), which the engine
already models and which cannot overwrite truth. This follows the manual's
"invisible until one of your ships hits a mine".

## Why
Fog of war is a stated rule; reports are an existing, tested seam.
