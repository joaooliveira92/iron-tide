# 06: Player sees mine situation and incidents

**What to build:** The Diplomacy & War screen shows, for areas of active wars,
the player's own laid pressure, own sweeping cover and suspected enemy pressure
from incident reports, plus recent incidents; incident reports raise the alerts
count. A fixture campaign with a strike shows it end to end.

**Blocked by:** 02, 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Enemy-laid pressure never appears except through incident reports.
- [ ] Reports show estimated cause, confidence and delay and are labelled as
      estimates.
- [ ] The alerts count rises with a new report.
- [ ] No effect on authoritative hashes from display preferences.
- [ ] Renderer and preload import no runtime effect library.
