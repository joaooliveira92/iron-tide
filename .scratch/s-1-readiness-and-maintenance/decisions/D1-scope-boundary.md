# D1: Scope boundary

**Type:** grilling · **Status:** resolved

## Question

What does S-1 own, and what are the seams left to other sprints?

## Answer

S-1 owns the per-ship readiness state and crew-quality value (representation,
seeding, compatibility), status-driven recurring maintenance, the repair premium and
wartime maintenance, and the battle-eligibility exclusion. Named seams for later
sprints:

- special training and the annual fleet exercise (pp.52-53) — their own effort;
- over-base deployment degrading maintenance and crew (p.45) — S-5;
- division experience and officer ability nudging crew quality (pp.55-57) — N-1
  ticket 07 / W-6;
- Raider / Foreign Station / Trade Protection assignments (p.48) — S-5 / W-1;
- repair capacity, docks and rebuilds (pp.31-32) — N-1 / F-3;
- national characteristics (Cautious and similar) — A-1 / S-4.

S-1 changes no existing maintenance constant before decision request 0007 resolves.

## Why

The plan's S-1 acceptance names exactly these mechanics ("Active/reserve/mothballed/
working-up, crew quality ramp, maintenance by status, repair cost ×2, wartime
maintenance; working-up ships excluded from battles"). The manual's neighbouring
maintenance/crew mechanics exist but sit on named sprints; pulling them in would
widen scope into deferred subsystems.
