# D-03: First increment — thin end-to-end vertical slice

**Type:** grilling
**Status:** resolved
**Blocked by:** None

## Question

Is the first deliverable the full 12-phase pipeline, or a thin end-to-end
vertical slice?

## Answer

**Thin end-to-end vertical slice** across the phases that already run:
economy, construction, research, and diplomacy — including persisted messages,
summary presentation, and at least **one representative decision interruption**
and **one representative battle interruption**. Phase coverage widens
incrementally afterward.

(Locked round 1, option a.)

## Consequence

Delivery is tracer-bullet per `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`: a narrow, complete,
demoable path first; never a flag-day 17-phase rewrite (see D-17). The
"representative" interruptions are backed by test/S-4-placeholder content, not an
authored catalogue (D-02, D-08).
