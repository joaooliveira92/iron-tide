Natural Earth terms of use, quoted from https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
(retrieved 2026-10-27; excerpts recorded in the Phase 0/1 G-01 report)

"All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public domain."
"No permission is needed to use Natural Earth."
"Crediting the authors is unnecessary."
Use is permitted "for personal, educational, and commercial purposes."

Caveat recorded by the same page: some underlying third-party data was licensed to Natural Earth
"for the sole purpose of creating a world base map". Only the land, minor-islands and rivers/lake
centrelines layers are consumed here. The marine-polygon and place layers are not used.

The README.html and VERSION.txt files shipped inside each pinned archive are copied verbatim into the
generated output directory.
