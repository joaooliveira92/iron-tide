## Findings

### What the manual grounds (class `manual`)

Pages 121-127 were re-read in full. They ground mechanics and families, and give exactly one date:

- Radar splits into **search radar** and **fire-control radar**; early search radar is unreliable; fire control "from level 3 will
  allow blind fire" (p.121). A nation "will receive a number of radar sets per month" once radar is invented (p.121).
- Missiles "will only start to have an impact in the game from circa 1945" (p.121). Types ASM, SSM, SAM, each Light, Medium or Heavy
  (pp.121-123); named real-world examples Talos, Terrier, Seaslug, Tartar, Seacat, He-293, KS-1 Komet, Exocet, Maverick, P-15
  Termit, Harpoon, Penguin, Gabriel (p.123).
- Special missiles modelled on the US Petrel (missile torpedo) and Swedish Rb 302/303 (diving missile) (p.126); the designer's note
  says unmatured projects exist in the game (p.124).
- Radar-directed medium AA, and close-in weapon systems for light AA, are upgrades in the missile age; more than 4 radar MAA per ship
  has diminishing returns, more than 8 is redundant (p.127). Data link and cruise missiles enable fire at targets seen by other
  ships (p.126). Peacetime missile storage has three levels (p.124).
- Aircraft operations are pp.102-120 (not re-read for this note; ticket 10 audits them).

The manual gives **no technology list, no per-technology year and no cost** for 1940-1970.

### What the community guide observes (class `community`)

From the Steam guide (line numbers refer to its extracted text): the density of radar technologies is highest 1939-1944, so radar
research dominates (l.275); carrier-borne jets unlock 1944 (l.276); heavy SSM destroyer conversions are feasible from 1947 (l.277);
carrier size limit and angled flight decks arrive 1950 (l.281); 30 kt carrier jet conversion with steam catapults 1952 (l.263, l.284);
enhanced sonar makes pre-existing ASW ships obsolete from 1955 (l.287); quadruple HSSM launchers 1959 and cruise missiles 1960 (l.299);
quadruple MSSM 1966 (l.307); missiles ignoring small ships about 1974 (l.295), beyond the horizon. These are the only RTW3-specific
year anchors found. None is from game data; each needs owner acceptance before use.

### Ship classes

The existing ship-type eras already name `MID_CENTURY` (1940) and `LATE` (1960) (`packages/ship-design/src/shipTypes.ts`), and the
dock-expansion era steps already cover 1940-1959 and 1960-1970. Postwar classes that follow from the sources above, as candidates: fleet
carrier (jet), escort carrier, guided-missile destroyer and frigate, ASW corvette and frigate, missile corvette and fast attack craft,
guided-missile cruiser. Their dates follow the technologies they require and are decided with them.
