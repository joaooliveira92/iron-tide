# Tactical battle glossary

The tactical battle context of Bluewave: how a fought battle is commanded and manoeuvred. The shared root vocabulary is
[CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

## Command

**Force**:
One side's group of divisions under a single flagship; it receives force orders.
_Avoid_: fleet (the campaign term), squadron

**Division**:
A group of one to five ships (destroyers up to six) that takes orders as one; the unit the player commands.
_Avoid_: squadron, group

**Lead division**:
The division that a non-independent division's role is relative to; the force flagship's division is always independent.
_Avoid_: parent division

**Role**:
How an AI-handled division behaves relative to its lead division: core, support, scout, screen, patrol, independent.
_Avoid_: mission, stance

**Formation**:
How a division's ships are stationed around its flagship: line ahead, line abreast, search line, screen.
_Avoid_: station keeping, order of battle

## Orders

**Division order**:
The player's instruction to one division: course or waypoint, speed, formation, role, lead division, control and turn together.
_Avoid_: unit order, command

**Stance**:
A named preset of a division order (engage, hold, withdraw) offered to the player as a shortcut.
_Avoid_: mode, posture

**Force order**:
An instruction to a whole force: flotilla attack, rally, disengage, change lead division, battle turn away.
_Avoid_: fleet order
